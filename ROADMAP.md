# Roadmap

A running list of what's planned. Items move to the release notes when they ship.

## Next release (v1.5)

- Pieces in spaced review, once Listen and score is reworked.

## Shipped

- **v1.4** — Spaced review: each scale, arpeggio and note gets its own review
  gap that grows with clean runs and resets after rough ones. Due today on the
  Progress tab, due counts on the tabs, Start review (scales with the mic, then
  a deck of due notes, then a summary), a Next review column, a Due soonest
  sort and a "Due for review" choice in flashcards. Fingering diagram: the left
  pinky keys are now laid out as you see them holding the horn (G♯ on top,
  C♯ outside and B inside, B♭ along the bottom).
- **v1.3** — Scales: melodic minor in all twelve keys, classical (raised 6th
  and 7th going up, natural minor coming down) and jazz (raised both ways).
  The scale staff now shows a natural sign when a note comes back after being
  sharpened or flattened earlier in the run. Listening on scales no longer
  jumps ahead while you hold a long note: a breath swell, a dropout or a
  pitch drift toward the next note used to count as a wrong next note.
- **v1.2** — Scales: with Listen with mic and Loop on, it keeps listening run
  after run with a run counter. Songs: Add your own is now a staff editor
  (click to place, per-note length / rest / accidental menu, drag to change
  pitch, bars fill themselves, undo, play from any note, paste notation as a
  fallback). QA regression suite in `qa/`.
- **v1.1** — Google sign-in and cross-device sync, practice log and weekly
  goal, faster pitch detection, fingering fixes.
- **v1.0** — Scales and arpeggios with mic listening, note flashcards, songs,
  proficiency tracking.
